Replicación de principios neurobiológicos en la inteligencia artificial: Aproximación al estado del arte

Published: July 15, 2026

Journal: Diversidad Académica
Universidad Autónoma del Estado de México

Professional Summary

This paper presents a documentary state-of-the-art review of neurobiological principles that inspired artificial neural networks. It analyzes concepts such as synaptic plasticity, distributed coding, neural pruning, hierarchical information processing, and compares biological neural systems with modern artificial intelligence models. The paper concludes that current neural networks are inspired by biological processes rather than direct replicas of human cognition, highlighting implications for AI education and interdisciplinary research.
