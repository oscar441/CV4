Replicación de principios neurobiológicos en la inteligencia artificial: Aproximación al estado del arte

Authors: Aidee Alejandra Velázquez Favela, Paola Monserrath Velázquez Favela, Oscar Salgado Flores

Journal: Diversidad Académica

Issue: Vol. 6 Núm. 1 (2026)

Publication date: July 15, 2026

Abstract: This article develops a documentary analysis of the state of the art regarding neurobiological principles that have inspired the design and operation of artificial neural networks used in artificial intelligence. The study examines biological processes such as synaptic plasticity, neuronal pruning, distributed coding, and hierarchical information organization, comparing them with computational mechanisms used in modern machine learning and deep learning models. The article concludes that artificial neural networks are inspired by neurobiology rather than direct replicas of human cognitive processes.

Keywords: artificial intelligence, neurobiology, artificial neural networks, machine learning, deep learning, education
