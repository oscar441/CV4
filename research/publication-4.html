Impacto de las redes sociales en la actividad académica y personal de los adolescentes. Valoraciones desde la perspectiva estudiantil

Published: January 15, 2025

Journal: Diversidad Académica
Universidad Autónoma del Estado de México

Professional Summary

This study analyzes how social media influences academic performance and personal life among high school students. Based on survey data collected from UAEM students, the research identifies excessive social media usage and discusses its educational implications.
