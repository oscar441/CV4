El derecho civil y familiar en México ante la infodemia. Retos y realidades

Published: January 15, 2026

Journal: Diversidad Académica
Universidad Autónoma del Estado de México

Professional Summary

This documentary research analyzes the impact of misinformation and information overload on Civil and Family Law in Mexico. The paper studies legal communication in digital environments, identifies major challenges for citizens and institutions, and proposes strategies to strengthen legal education and responsible communication.
