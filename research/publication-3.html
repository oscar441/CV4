Published: July 15, 2025

Professional Summary

This literature review examines how artificial intelligence is transforming students' cognitive, digital, metacognitive, and socio-emotional skills. It discusses both educational opportunities and risks associated with AI adoption while emphasizing ethical and critical implementation in educational environments.
