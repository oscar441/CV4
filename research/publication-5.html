Diseño de plantilla interactiva autoevaluable de Google Drive sin conexión a internet para estudiantes de preparatoria

Published: July 15, 2024

Journal: Diversidad Académica
Universidad Autónoma del Estado de México

Professional Summary

This work presents the design and implementation of an offline self-assessment template using Google Sheets for high school students. Developed using Agile SDLC practices, the solution allows automatic feedback without Internet access and demonstrates practical educational software engineering.
